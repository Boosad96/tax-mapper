Which law applies to you?

Returns for FY 2025-26 (assessment year 2026-27) are filed under the old 1961 Act — old section and form numbers. Income from 1 April 2026 onward (Tax Year 2026-27) is under the Income-tax Act, 2025 — new numbers. Pending assessments, notices and appeals for years before 1 April 2026 continue under the 1961 Act. TDS follows the earlier of the payment or credit date.

Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 80C?
Section 123 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Deductions for LIC, PPF, ELSS, tuition fees and housing-loan principal moved from Sections 80C/80CCC to Section 123.
When does the Income-tax Act, 2025 apply?
From 1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27). Returns for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) are still filed under the 1961 Act with old numbers.
Did the tax rates change?
No. Rates, slabs and limits are unchanged — only section, form and schedule numbers moved.
Where is the official mapping?
The CBDT published a section-wise concordance (Form 121) and the e-filing portal hosts a comparison utility: “Utility to check provisions of Income-tax Act, 1961 vis-à-vis Income-tax Act, 2025” at incometax.gov.in.
Is this tax advice?
No. This is a free reference tool. Verify any number against the enacted Act or the official CBDT concordance before filing.
