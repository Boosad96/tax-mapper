TDS on salary

Old Act (1961)Section 192
New Act (2025)Section 392
Applies from1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27). Returns for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) still use the old number.

Salary did NOT move into Section 393 with the rest of TDS — it has its own section.

Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 192?
Section 392 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. Salary TDS has its own section — it did not move into Section 393 with the rest of TDS.
What are the TDS payment codes for salary?
1001 (state/local government), 1002 (private sector), 1003 (Union government), and 1004 (premature EPF withdrawal, old Section 192A).
