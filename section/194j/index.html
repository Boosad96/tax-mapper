Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 194J?
Section 393(1) Sl.6(iii) of the Income-tax Act, 2025.
What are the TDS codes for 194J?
1026 for technical services and royalty (2%), 1027 for professional services (10%), and 1028 for director fees (10%, no threshold). Threshold for 1026/1027: ₹50,000 per year.
