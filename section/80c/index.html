Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 80C?
Section 123 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. It covers the same deductions — LIC, PPF, ELSS, tuition fees and housing-loan principal — with the ₹1.5 lakh limit unchanged.
Does 80C still exist in the new Act?
Not by that number. The old Chapter VI-A deductions (80C–80U) now live in Sections 122–154 of the 2025 Act. 80C specifically is Section 123.
