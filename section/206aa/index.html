Old Section 206AA → New Section 397(2)

Higher TDS where PAN is not furnished

Old Act (1961)Section 206AA
New Act (2025)Section 397(2)
Applies from1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27). Returns for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) still use the old number.

Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 206AA?
Section 397(2) of the Income-tax Act, 2025. TDS at 20% or the applicable rate, whichever is higher, when PAN is not furnished.
