Section Mapper › Section 234A

Old Section 234A → New Section 423

Interest for late filing of return

Old Act (1961)Section 234A
New Act (2025)Section 423
Applies from1 April 2026 (Tax Year 2026-27). Returns for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) still use the old number.

One source cites Section 430 instead; the majority say 423.
