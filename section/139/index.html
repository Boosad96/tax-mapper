Frequently asked questions

What is the new section for 139?
Section 263 of the Income-tax Act, 2025. It covers original, belated, revised and updated returns.
Which Act applies to returns for FY 2025-26?
The 1961 Act. Returns for FY 2025-26 (AY 2026-27) are filed under the old Act with old section numbers. The 2025 Act applies to income from 1 April 2026 onward.
